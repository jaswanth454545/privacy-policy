1. Introduction

Welcome to MC Vegetables (also known as "KaikariXpress"), a mobile application operated by MC Vegetables ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

By downloading, installing, or using MC Vegetables, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the application.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Account Management: To create and manage your account, authenticate your identity via phone OTP verification.
  • Order Fulfillment: To process, deliver, and track your vegetable orders to the correct address within the selected delivery slot.
  • Payment Processing: To facilitate online payments, Cash on Delivery, and Pay Later transactions through Cashfree payment gateway.
  • Location Services: To determine your delivery zone, show nearby stores, auto-detect your address, and ensure you are within our serviceable area.
  • Wallet Management: To manage your in-app wallet balance, credits, debits, and outstanding dues.
  • Price Negotiation: To facilitate "Best Price" requests and negotiations between buyers and sellers.
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries and resolve issues related to orders, payments, or account.
  • App Improvement: To understand how you use our app and improve our services, features, and user experience.

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

Certain data is stored locally on your device using secure on-device storage (AsyncStorage) for offline functionality and faster app performance. This includes your profile information, saved addresses, and recent order history.

4.2 Server-Side Storage

Your account data, order history, wallet balance, and transaction records are stored on our secure backend servers hosted at api.kaikarixpress.com. Communication between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS protocols.

4.3 Firebase Services

We use Google Firebase for phone number authentication. Firebase processes your phone number for OTP verification and may use Google Play Integrity / reCAPTCHA for app verification security. Firebase's own privacy policy applies to data processed by Firebase services: Firebase Privacy Policy.

4.4 Security Measures

  • Phone number and Firebase UID-based authentication for all API requests
  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Session validation — requiring both active Firebase auth and stored phone number
  • Automatic session cleanup on sign-out (all locally cached auth data is cleared)
  • No plain-text storage of passwords (the app uses passwordless OTP authentication)

6. App Permissions

MC Vegetables requests the following device permissions:

Permission Why We Need It
Internet Access Required to communicate with our servers, process orders, and handle payments
Fine Location (GPS) To accurately detect your location for delivery address auto-fill and nearby store discovery
Coarse Location (Network) Fallback location detection using WiFi and cellular networks when GPS is unavailable

📍 Location permission is requested only when needed and you can deny or revoke it at any time from your device settings. The app will still function, but address auto-detection will be unavailable.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Delivery: With delivery personnel to fulfill your orders (limited to delivery address and order details).
  • Payment Processing: With Cashfree Payments to process your online transactions.
  • Authentication: With Google Firebase for phone number verification.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with any merger, acquisition, or sale of company assets.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services. Specifically:

  • Account Data: Retained until you request account deletion.
  • Order History: Retained for a minimum of 3 years for business and legal compliance.
  • Transaction Records: Retained as required by applicable financial regulations.
  • Local Device Data: Cleared immediately upon sign-out or app uninstallation.

9. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: You can view your profile, order history, and wallet data within the app at any time.
  • Correction: You can update your name and email through the Edit Profile screen in the app.
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us (see Section 12).
  • Revoke Permissions: You can revoke location and other permissions through your device settings at any time.
  • Data Portability: You can request a copy of your personal data by contacting us.
  • Sign Out: You can sign out at any time, which clears all locally cached authentication data from your device.

10. Children's Privacy

MC Vegetables is not intended for use by children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 18 without parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information from our servers.
