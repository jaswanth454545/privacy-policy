This action is permanent. Once your account is deleted, all your personal data, order history, wallet balance, and saved addresses will be permanently removed from our systems. This action cannot be undone.

How to Delete Your Account

You can request account deletion using any of the following methods:

Method 1: From Within the App

1

Open Settings

Go to Profile → Settings in the MC Vegetables app.

2

Navigate to Privacy & Security

Scroll down to the "Privacy & Security" section.

3

Tap "Delete Account"

Tap on "Delete Account" (shown in red). Confirm when prompted. Your account and all associated data will be scheduled for deletion.

Method 2: Submit a Request Below

If you cannot access the app or prefer to request deletion through this page, use the form below.

Method 3: Email Us Directly

Send an email to support@kaikarixpress.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include your registered phone number.

What Data Will Be Deleted

When you request account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed:

Data Type Action Timeline
Account profile (name, email, phone) Deleted Within 7 days
Saved delivery addresses Deleted Within 7 days
Wallet balance & transaction history Deleted Within 7 days
Cart & wishlist data Deleted Immediately
Price negotiation history Deleted Within 7 days
App preferences & notification settings Deleted Immediately
Firebase authentication data Deleted Within 7 days
Order history & transaction records Retained* Retained for 3 years
Payment records (Cashfree) Retained* Per financial regulations

* Order and payment records are retained in anonymized form as required by Indian financial regulations (GST compliance, income tax records). Personal identifiers are removed from these records.

Important Things to Know

  • Outstanding Dues: Please clear any pending dues or payments before requesting deletion. Accounts with outstanding financial obligations may not be immediately eligible for deletion.
  • Active Orders: If you have active/pending orders, please wait for them to be delivered or cancelled before requesting account deletion.
  • Wallet Balance: Any remaining wallet balance will be forfeited upon account deletion. Please use your balance or contact support for a refund before deleting.
  • Re-registration: After deletion, you may create a new account using the same phone number. However, none of your previous data (orders, wallet, addresses) will be recoverable.
  • Processing Time: Account deletion requests are processed within 7 business days. You will not be able to use the app during this period.

🤔 Before You Delete — Consider These Alternatives

  • Sign Out: Simply sign out from the app if you want to stop using it temporarily. Your data stays safe and you can sign back in anytime.
  • Disable Notifications: Go to Settings → Notifications to turn off push notifications instead of deleting your account.
  • Clear Cache: Go to Settings → Clear Cache to free up space without losing your account.
  • Contact Support: If you're facing issues, our support team might be able to help resolve them.
